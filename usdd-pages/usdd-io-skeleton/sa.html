Smart Allocator: USDD's Sustainable Yield Strategy

Smart Allocator is USDD's yield-sharing initiative, where capital from USDD's cash reserve is deployed into diverse investment opportunities to earn returns in the form of interest and platform rewards. The profits are distributed to users through USDD Earn.

Our investment strategies include supplying USDD to trusted DeFi protocols to earn interest, as well as capturing funding fees by deploying USDD on exchanges that offer perpetual futures.

The chosen investment platforms are selected by the USDD and JUST DAO teams based on strict risk controls, and all investment activities are publicly viewable on this data page.
