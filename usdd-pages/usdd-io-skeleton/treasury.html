USDD Treasury Dashboard

The USDD Treasury Dashboard provides full transparency into our financial performance and ecosystem initiatives. It is updated on a quarterly basis. As the JST ecosystem continues to evolve, its governance token JST plays an increasingly vital role in both JustLend DAO and USDD.

To optimize token economics, secure long-term value, and enhance governance efficiency, the community has launched a JST buyback and burn mechanism.

  • The buyback program will be funded primarily by two sources: JustLend DAO's net revenue and USDD ecosystem revenue. After repaying the subsidy provided by TRON DAO, USDD ecosystem revenue exceeding $10 million will be used for buybacks.
  • All buyback operations will be executed transparently on-chain.
  • This approach will progressively reduce the circulating supply of JST in the market, creating a deflationary model that adds value and drives the JST ecosystem towards greater sustainability.
