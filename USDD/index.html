Better Attestations

Compared with other fiat-backed stablecoins, TUSD provides attestation reports at shorter intervals — specifically, on a daily basis. These reports provide transparency into the state of TUSD's reserves as well as its reserve breakdown. The attestation process is executed by Moore Hong Kong, a global leading accounting firm.
