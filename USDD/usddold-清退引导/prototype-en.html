About PSM

What is PSM?

PSM (Peg Stability Module) is a stablecoin swap tool launched by the TRON DAO Reserve. Users can swap between USDDOLD and other stablecoins at a 1:1 ratio.

Why PSM?

Unlike regular swap platforms, PSM offers more stable and competitive price with no slippage and zero fee.

How does PSM work?

As the latest monetary policy tool launched by the TRON DAO Reserve, PSM helps keep the USDDOLD peg close to $1 U.S. dollar during times when USDDOLD demand outstrips USDDOLD supply.
