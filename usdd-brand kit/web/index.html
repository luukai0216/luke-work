Common Mistakes

Common mistakes to avoid when using the USDD logo. When in doubt, use assets as provided in the brand kit without modification.

Don't change the color. Use colors from the brand palette.

Don't delete or alter elements of the logo.

Don't tilt or rotate the logo.

Don't reduce the logo's opacity or apply a transparent tint.

Don't crop the logo or place it inside a square mask.

Don't add shadows, glows, or other visual effects.

Don't flip, mirror, or otherwise modify the graphic.

Don't squeeze, stretch, or change the logo's aspect ratio.
