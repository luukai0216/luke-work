06 · Usage Guidelines

Common Mistakes

Common mistakes to avoid when using the USDD logo. When in doubt, use assets as provided in the brand kit without modification.

DON'T Don't change the color. Use colors from the brand palette.
DON'T Don't crop the logo or place it inside a square mask.
DON'T Don't delete or alter elements of the logo.
DON'T Don't add shadows, glows, or other visual effects.
DON'T Don't tilt or rotate the logo.
DON'T Don't flip, mirror, or otherwise modify the graphic.
DON'T Don't reduce the logo's opacity or apply a transparent tint.
DON'T Don't squeeze, stretch, or change the logo's aspect ratio.
